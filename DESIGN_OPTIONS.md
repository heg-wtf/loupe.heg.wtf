# Loupe — MZ landing concepts

## 01. Neo Contact Sheet — selected

Warm off-white editorial canvas, near-black type, acid-lime stickers, coral accents, oversized condensed headlines, and a contact-sheet photo grid. The polished graphite/emerald Loupe icon anchors the playful layout so it still feels like a paid Mac product.

- Mood: indie magazine × photo lab × Gen-Z sticker collage
- Strength: explains “search your own photos” immediately and feels distinctive
- Motion: ticker, gentle sticker float, search-result reveal
- Palette: `#F3F0E8`, `#181817`, `#C7FF4A`, `#FF6B57`, `#4CB782`

## 02. Emerald After Dark

Near-black background, emerald glow, translucent Liquid Glass cards, cinematic photo strips, and restrained chrome details.

- Mood: premium Mac utility × night gallery
- Strength: strongest privacy/premium signal
- Trade-off: visually familiar among AI products; less playful

## 03. Soft Flash Club

Powder lavender and sky blue, rounded bubble typography, flash-photo frames, hand-drawn arrows, and scrapbook notes.

- Mood: Y2K digicam × social scrapbook
- Strength: youthful and social-first
- Trade-off: can undersell a privacy-focused professional Mac utility

## Direction

Implement **Neo Contact Sheet**, borrowing only the emerald glass depth from concept 02. The result keeps the MZ/editorial energy while respecting the real app icon and macOS-native product quality.
