Privacy,
plainly.

Effective August 31, 2026 · Loupe for macOS · HEG Inc.

The short version

Loupe’s photo indexing and search run entirely on your Mac. Your photos, search text, and local search index never leave your Mac and are never sent to HEG Inc. Loupe has no account, advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDK.

What Loupe accesses

With your permission, Loupe reads your Apple Photos library to build a local search index and display results. No photo, thumbnail, or index data is uploaded.

Apple system services

Two optional features use Apple services. Place names use CLGeocoder, and map previews use MapKit. Photos containing GPS metadata may therefore send coordinates to Apple to return a place name or map tile. HEG does not receive that data.

Website and purchase

This website does not use advertising or analytics. When you choose to buy Loupe, checkout is provided by Lemon Squeezy, the merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy processes the information needed for payment, tax, fraud prevention, receipts, and download delivery under its privacy policy. HEG may receive order details needed for customer support and fulfillment, but never your complete card number.

Data we collect in the app

None. The app does not transmit personal data to HEG.

Children

Loupe does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
