FAQ

Does Loupe upload my photos?

No. Photo indexing and search happen entirely on your Mac. Photos, queries, and the search index are not sent to HEG.

Which Macs are supported?

Loupe currently requires macOS 26 or later.

How do I download my purchase?

Loupe is sold on the Mac App Store. After purchase it installs on your Mac, and you can re-download it any time from the App Store’s Purchased list with the same Apple Account.

Is this a subscription?

No. Loupe is a $14.99 one-time purchase.
